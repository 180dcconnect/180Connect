## 2. Purpose of this Agreement

This proposed agreement establishes the data protection and governance arrangements between Sheffield CIC and 180DC Global in relation to:
* The `180dc.org` Google Workspace environment;
* The `sheffield@180dc.org` mailbox used by the Sheffield branch;
* Personal data contained within that mailbox;
* Personal data processed through 180Connect and transmitted to or from the mailbox; and
* Global's administrative, technical, security, and support access to that environment.

The purpose of this agreement is to ensure that the Parties have a clear and aligned understanding of:
* Who is responsible for the processing;
* What access Global has;
* What Global may and may not do with Sheffield's data;
* How international access is governed;
* How data-subject requests are handled;
* How security incidents are handled; and
* How the arrangement is reviewed.

---

## 3. Description of the Relationship

### 3.1 Sheffield's Role
Sheffield CIC is responsible for the operation of 180Connect and determines the purposes and means of personal-data processing carried out through the platform for Sheffield's client-outreach activities.

This includes decisions concerning:
* Which external sources are used;
* Which organisations are researched;
* Which categories of information are collected;
* Which professional contact information is retained;
* How information is enriched and classified;
* Which organisations are prioritised for outreach;
* How outreach is conducted;
* How contact history is maintained;
* Retention and deletion of 180Connect records; and
* The lawful basis and transparency arrangements applicable to Sheffield's outreach processing.

Accordingly, **Sheffield CIC considers itself the data controller** for the 180Connect outreach processing described above, subject to confirmation of the Parties' respective roles through this agreement.

### 3.2 Global's Role
Based on the current operating arrangement, Global provides the following functions:
* Workspace administration;
* Account provisioning and management;
* Technical support;
* Cybersecurity and security administration;
* Administration of the Google Workspace environment;
* Investigation and response to Workspace-level security incidents; and
* Maintenance of the wider infrastructure through which the Sheffield mailbox operates.

Global administrators have elevated administrative privileges within the Workspace environment and may technically access the Sheffield mailbox and its contents where required for these purposes.

### 3.3 Scope of Global's Access
For the purposes of this proposed arrangement, Global's administrative access does not by itself determine the purposes of Sheffield's 180Connect outreach. Sheffield independently determines how 180Connect is used for its outreach activities.

Global's access to Sheffield outreach data is therefore proposed to be limited to what is reasonably necessary for:
* Workspace administration;
* Technical support;
* Cybersecurity;
* Incident investigation;
* Account management;
* Compliance with applicable legal obligations; and
* Other infrastructure-management purposes agreed between the Parties.

### 3.4 Confirmation of Roles
The Parties acknowledge that the precise data-protection roles applicable to Global's access to Sheffield personal data are to be confirmed through review of this agreement and the Parties' actual processing arrangements.

The Parties will determine, for each relevant processing activity, whether Global is acting:
* As a processor on behalf of Sheffield CIC;
* As a separate controller for its own independent purposes; or
* As a joint controller with Sheffield CIC where the Parties jointly determine the purposes and means of the relevant processing.

The Parties will document the agreed position and, where required, update this agreement and any associated data-protection or international-transfer documentation before the relevant processing begins or materially changes. Nothing in this draft is intended to predetermine the legal classification before that review has been completed.

## 4. Use of Sheffield Outreach Data by Global

Subject to Global's review and approval of this agreement, the proposed arrangement is that Global will not use Sheffield's 180Connect outreach data for an independent organisational purpose merely because Global has technical or administrative access to the Workspace environment.

In particular, Global will not, without prior assessment and agreement:
* Use Sheffield outreach contacts for Global's own marketing;
* Use the data to conduct separate outreach;
* Add Sheffield outreach contacts to another branch's outreach database;
* Share the data with another 180DC branch for its own outreach purposes;
* Use the data for unrelated analytics or profiling; or
* Otherwise repurpose the information for an independent purpose.

If Global wishes to use Sheffield outreach data for an independent purpose, the Parties must first determine the appropriate data-protection relationship and lawful basis for that processing. The relevant processing may require Global to act as an independent controller or the Parties to establish another appropriate arrangement. New processing must not begin until appropriate assessments and documentation are completed.

## 5. Workspace Administration and Confidentiality

Global administrators may have technical access to mailbox contents even where such access is not required for ordinary administration.

Where access to message contents is necessary:
* Access must be limited to the minimum reasonably necessary;
* The administrator must have a legitimate administrative, security, legal, or support reason;
* Information accessed must be treated as confidential;
* Information must not be copied or reused for unrelated purposes; and
* Information must not be disclosed to another organisation or 180DC branch unless authorised under an appropriate legal and data-protection basis.

The Parties acknowledge that Global's super-admin privileges create a technical capability for access that cannot be fully removed by Sheffield. The purpose of this agreement is therefore to establish the legal, organisational, and procedural restrictions governing that capability, rather than to represent that the technical capability does not exist.

## 6. International Data Access – Australia

The Parties acknowledge that:
* Sheffield CIC is established in the United Kingdom;
* 180DC Global is established in Australia;
* Global administrators who may access the Sheffield Workspace environment are located primarily in Australia, with at least one administrator located in the UK; and
* Australian-based administrators may technically access personal information contained within the Sheffield mailbox.

The Parties acknowledge that remote access to UK personal data by Global personnel located in Australia may constitute a restricted transfer under UK data-protection law. The Parties will therefore assess and document the applicable transfer requirements before relying on Australian access as an ongoing processing arrangement.

Where a restricted transfer is identified, the Parties will implement the appropriate transfer mechanism required by applicable UK data-protection law, which may include an International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or another applicable safeguard, supported by an appropriate transfer risk assessment where required.

The final transfer documentation will specify:
* The categories of personal data transferred;
* The purpose of access;
* The categories of individuals concerned;
* The Australian recipients;
* The locations from which access occurs;
* The technical security measures applied;
* Onward-transfer restrictions;
* Data-subject rights; and
* The applicable contractual safeguards.

## 7. Data Minimisation

Sheffield will ensure that only personal data necessary for 180Connect's legitimate outreach purposes is retained in the system.

The Parties acknowledge that:
* Personal home addresses are excluded;
* Personal telephone numbers are excluded;
* Personal email addresses are excluded;
* Special-category data is not intentionally collected;
* Generic organisational role contact addresses (`info@`, `contact@`, `fundraising@`) are retained where appropriate; and
* Unnecessary personal information returned by external sources is filtered before storage.

Global will not request or require additional personal information from Sheffield merely because such information may be technically available through the Workspace environment.

## 8. Security Responsibilities

### 8.1 Sheffield Responsibilities
Sheffield is responsible for:
* 180Connect application security;
* Access controls within 180Connect;
* Data minimisation and automated field exclusions;
* Suppression and opt-out controls;
* Retention and deletion within 180Connect;
* Lawful basis documentation (LIA);
* Transparency and privacy notices;
* Handling ordinary data-subject requests concerning Sheffield's outreach processing; and
* Maintaining the 180Connect internal breach log.

### 8.2 Global Responsibilities
Global is responsible for:
* Administration of the Google Workspace environment;
* Workspace-level security controls;
* Management and access governance of Global administrator accounts;
* Responding to Workspace-level security incidents;
* Providing relevant Workspace logs/information to Sheffield where necessary for an investigation; and
* Maintaining appropriate confidentiality over information accessed through administrative privileges.

---

## 9. Data Subject Requests

The Parties agree that **Sheffield CIC will be the primary point of contact** for data-subject requests concerning Sheffield's 180Connect outreach processing.

Where an individual contacts Sheffield concerning personal information processed through 180Connect:
* Sheffield will receive, log, and assess the request;
* Sheffield will respond in accordance with UK GDPR (within 1 calendar month);
* Sheffield will provide the information it holds directly where appropriate;
* Sheffield will not routinely refer ordinary requests to Global;
* Where a request concerns information requiring Workspace-level intervention, Sheffield will escalate the relevant portion to Global;
* Global will provide prompt and reasonable assistance where information is within Global's administrative control; and
* Sheffield remains responsible for coordinating the response to the individual.

---

## 10. Security Incidents and Personal Data Breaches

### 10.1 Sheffield Incidents
Where a security incident originates within 180Connect, Sheffield will:
* Contain the incident;
* Investigate its scope and cause;
* Revoke affected credentials;
* Record the incident in the breach log;
* Assess whether it constitutes a personal data breach; and
* Determine whether notification to the ICO (within 72 hours) or affected individuals is required under UK GDPR Articles 33/34.

### 10.2 Workspace Incidents
Where an incident concerns the Google Workspace environment or the Sheffield mailbox:
* Sheffield will take immediate steps available to contain the issue;
* Sheffield will notify Global where Global intervention is necessary;
* Global will investigate the Workspace-level aspect of the incident;
* Global will provide Sheffield with relevant information to assess the incident;
* The Parties will cooperate to determine whether personal data has been affected;
* Sheffield will assess its UK GDPR notification obligations; and
* The Parties will cooperate with any required regulatory or data-subject notifications.

---

## 11. Data Retention and Deletion

Sheffield's 180Connect retention periods are governed by the 180Connect Data Handling Policy and Data Lifecycle Policy.

Global will not establish a separate retention period for Sheffield's outreach data unless required for legitimate Workspace administration, security, legal, or technical reasons.

Where Sheffield requests deletion of data that is within the scope of Global's administrative control, Global will provide reasonable assistance subject to technical limitations, legal retention requirements, and security policies.

---

## 12. Access Control

Global will maintain strict controls over its super-admin accounts:
* Restricted to authorised Global personnel;
* Protected by multi-factor authentication (MFA);
* Individual, unshared credentials;
* Periodically reviewed; and
* Revoked promptly when an administrator changes roles or departs.

---

## 13. Changes to Workspace Administration

Global retains administrative authority over the `180dc.org` Google Workspace environment, including the ability to manage accounts, permissions, security controls, and other Workspace-level infrastructure.

Where a material change to Workspace administration could materially affect Sheffield's processing of personal data, Global will, where reasonably practicable, notify Sheffield in advance or as soon as reasonably practicable after the change.

This does not prevent Global from taking immediate action where necessary to protect the security, integrity, or availability of the Workspace environment.

Where a Workspace-level change materially alters the international access, processing purposes, categories of data, or security arrangements applicable to Sheffield's personal data, the Parties will review this agreement and any associated transfer or data-protection documentation.

---

## 14. Onward Disclosure

Global must not disclose Sheffield's outreach personal data to another 180DC branch, external organisation, service provider, or Global team for a new or independent purpose unless the disclosure is authorised under applicable data protection law and appropriate transfer safeguards are in place.

---

## 15. Subprocessors and Third-Party Infrastructure

Nothing in this agreement changes the existing processor arrangements between Sheffield and third-party infrastructure providers (such as Google LLC, Supabase Inc., Vercel Inc., Resend Inc., PostHog Inc.). Each provider is documented in Sheffield's processor register in accordance with the Data Handling Policy.

---

## 16. Cooperation and Information

The Parties will cooperate reasonably where necessary to demonstrate compliance with applicable data-protection requirements, including SARs, breaches, international transfers, audits, and regulatory inquiries.

---

## 17. Changes to Processing

Sheffield will notify Global if a material change to 180Connect substantially alters the nature or scale of personal data processing within the Workspace environment. Global will similarly notify Sheffield if a material change to Workspace affects Sheffield's data processing.

---

## 18. Independent Purposes

Nothing in this agreement prevents either organisation from acting as an independent controller for personal data that it processes for its own legitimate organisational purposes. However, using the other party's data for new independent purposes requires prior assessment of lawful bases, roles, and transfer safeguards.

---

## 19. Review

This agreement will be reviewed at least annually, upon material changes to Workspace or 180Connect, or following a significant security incident.
