## 1. Who We Are

**180 Degrees Consulting Sheffield CIC** ("we", "us", "our") is an independent Community Interest Company registered in England and Wales (Company No. `15874544`), operating as a university-affiliated branch of the global 180 Degrees Consulting network. 

We provide management consultancy, strategic planning, impact measurement, and operational advisory services to registered charities, Community Interest Companies, and social enterprises, primarily staffed by trained student volunteer consultants under professional mentorship.

---

## 2. Scope of this Notice

This Notice applies to individuals acting in their **professional, executive, or governance capacity** (such as trustees, directors, chief executives, and fundraising managers) on behalf of UK non-profit and social-sector organisations whose contact details have been obtained **indirectly from public sources** for prospective B2B client acquisition.

If you have received an introductory email from our team (`sheffield@180dc.org`), this notice provides full transparency regarding how your professional data was gathered, why it is processed, and your absolute right to object.

## 3. Categories of Personal Data We Process

We collect and process limited professional information necessary to evaluate whether our advisory services are relevant to your organisation:

### 3.1 Permitted Professional Data
* **Professional Identifiers:** Your full name, job title, and organisational role.
* **Business Contact Details:** Professional email address (role inboxes e.g. `info@`, `contact@`, or publicly listed corporate email), business telephone number, and registered office address.
* **Organisational Context:** Name of your organisation, charity/company registration numbers, sector classification, declared charitable objectives, financial scale, and geographical operational area.
* **Outreach History:** Records of introductory emails sent, delivery timestamps, responses received, and do-not-contact suppression preferences.

### 3.2 Strictly Excluded Data (Data Minimisation)
We enforce automated filters prohibiting the collection or storage of:
* ❌ Personal residential/home addresses;
* ❌ Private/personal telephone numbers;
* ❌ Personal email addresses (Gmail, Hotmail, Yahoo, or personal ISP accounts);
* ❌ Dates of birth, nationality, or family details;
* ❌ Special category personal data (health, ethnicity, religion, political beliefs, sexual orientation);
* ❌ Criminal records or personal financial data.

## 6. Automated Decision-Support & Profiling (Article 14(2)(g))

Our internal platform (180Connect) calculates data-completeness and organisational suitability priority scores based on public organisation attributes (income band, sector, location).

* **Decision-Support Only:** Priority scores are internal recommendation signals used solely to help Client Acquisition Managers (CAMs) organise their research.
* **No Solely Automated Decisions:** The platform does not take automated actions, send emails autonomously, or make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on individuals. Every communication is independently reviewed, drafted, and initiated by a human team member.

## 8. International Transfers & Safeguards

Where personal data is transferred or accessed outside the United Kingdom:
* **European Economic Area (EEA):** Transfers to EU-based processors (e.g. Supabase in Ireland) rely on the UK Government's adequacy regulations.
* **Third Countries (e.g. US, Australia):** Transfers or remote administrative access rely on standard contractual safeguards, specifically the **UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA)** or **UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)**, supported by Transfer Risk Assessments (TRAs).

---

## 9. Data Retention & Deletion

* **Raw Registry Ingestion Payloads:** Purged automatically after **12 months**.
* **Prospective Organisation Records:** Retained for up to **24 months** from last contact if no engagement occurs, after which contact records are reviewed and refreshed or deleted.
* **Permanent Suppression Records:** Stored **indefinitely** in a dedicated suppression table (`email_address`, `timestamp`, `reason`) to guarantee that opted-out contacts are never re-contacted.
* **Database Backups:** Securely rotated and purged after **30 days**.

## 10. Your Data Protection Rights

Under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, you hold statutory rights regarding your personal data:

1. **Right of Access (Article 15):** You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
2. **Right to Rectification (Article 16):** You have the right to request that inaccurate or incomplete contact information be updated.
3. **Right to Erasure (Article 17):** You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data where there is no overriding legal basis for its retention.
4. **Right to Restriction (Article 18):** You have the right to request that we pause processing of your data while an objection or accuracy claim is investigated.
5. **ABSOLUTE RIGHT TO OBJECT TO DIRECT MARKETING (Article 21(2)):**
   > **You have an absolute, unconditional right to object at any time to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing purposes.**  
   > If you object, we will immediately cease sending outreach communications and add your address to our permanent suppression register.
