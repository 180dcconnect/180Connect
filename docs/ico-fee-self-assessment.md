## 1. Context & Purpose

Under the Data Protection (Charges and Information) Regulations 2018, every data controller in the UK processing personal data electronically must pay an annual data protection fee to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), unless they qualify for a specific statutory exemption.

This document formally records the answers, context, and legal outcome of the official ICO Data Protection Fee self-assessment completed for 180 Degrees Consulting Sheffield CIC in relation to its operations and the 180Connect platform.

## 3. Official ICO Outcome

* **Determination:** **Required to pay the data protection fee.**
* **Fee Tier:** **Tier 1** (micro-organisation: maximum 10 members of staff, or annual turnover of £632,000 or less).
* **Standard Annual Fee:** **£52**
* **Direct Debit Discounted Fee:** **£47** (the ICO applies a standing £5 discount when paid by Direct Debit).
